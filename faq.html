Frequently Asked Questions

How can I hire a freelancer?

You can create an account, search for skilled freelancers, check their profiles, and send a project request.

How do I post a project?

After registration, go to the Projects section, click on "Post Project", add requirements, budget and submit.

How do I select the right freelancer?

You can check freelancer ratings, previous projects, skills, experience, and client reviews before hiring.

Can I communicate with freelancers before hiring?

Yes, FreelanceHub provides a messaging system where clients and freelancers can discuss project requirements.

Freelancer Questions

How can I become a freelancer?

Register as a freelancer, complete your profile, add skills, portfolio and start applying for projects.

How can I get projects?

Browse available projects and submit professional proposals according to your skills and experience.

How do freelancer ratings work?

Clients can rate freelancers after project completion. Higher ratings improve your profile visibility.

Payment & Security

How are payments processed?

Payments are processed securely through our platform after project milestones are completed.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, FreelanceHub uses secure payment methods to protect user information and transactions.

Can I get a refund?

Refund policies depend on project status, agreements and platform guidelines.
